About us

About StaneAI

StaneAI (also written Stane AI) is an AI and mobile app development company in India. We turn ideas into useful, intelligent products — mobile apps, AI automation, AI chatbots and custom software.

Who we are

StaneAI Private Limited is a private limited AI and software products company founded in 2026 and registered in India. We are a small studio with a product mindset: we care less about technology for its own sake and more about whether the thing we build makes someone’s day simpler. Our main focus is mobile applications for Android and iOS, alongside AI-powered workflows, chatbots and custom software.

We work with startups, founders and small and growing businesses in India, and with businesses in the United States.

What we do

  • Mobile App Development — Android and iOS apps, cross-platform or native, from a focused MVP to launch and beyond.
  • AI & Workflow Automation — removing repetitive work by connecting your tools and letting AI handle the messy steps.
  • AI Chatbot Development — chatbots that answer real customer questions from your own information, with a clear hand-over to people.
  • Software Development — web applications, dashboards and internal tools built around how your business works.

What we believe

  • Product-led. Build for real people. Start with the problem and the outcome that matters, not the feature list.
  • AI-enabled. Automate with purpose. Use AI where it removes real effort, and keep people in the loop where mistakes would be costly.
  • Mobile-first. Designed for daily life. Most people meet a product on their phone, so that is where we start.

How we work

  1. Understand — start with the problem, the people and the outcome that matters.
  2. Explore — shape ideas into clear concepts and practical product directions.
  3. Build — turn the chosen direction into a thoughtful digital experience.
  4. Improve — learn from real use, refine and keep making the product more useful.
