Mobile App Development for Android & iOS

Mobile apps are our main focus. StaneAI designs and builds Android and iOS apps that are simple to use, fast and ready for real-world use — from the first sketch to launch on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

What we build

  • Android apps for phones and tablets, published on Google Play.
  • iOS apps for iPhone and iPad, published on the Apple App Store.
  • Cross-platform apps that share one codebase across Android and iOS when that is the right trade-off.
  • AI-powered app features such as smart search, recommendations, chat assistants and document or image understanding.
  • MVPs — a focused first version to test an idea with real users before investing more.

Native or cross-platform?

There is no single right answer. Native development (Kotlin for Android, Swift for iOS) gives the best access to device features and performance. Cross-platform frameworks such as Flutter or React Native let one team ship to both platforms faster. We recommend an approach based on your features, budget, timeline and who your users are — not on habit.

How we work

  1. Understand — the problem, the users and what success looks like.
  2. Design — user flows and screens you can click through before code is written.
  3. Build — in short iterations, so you see working software early and often.
  4. Test — on real devices and screen sizes, not just emulators.
  5. Launch — store listings, screenshots, privacy details and review submission.
  6. Improve — updates based on feedback, crash reports and usage.

Why StaneAI

We are a product-first company: we are building our own portfolio of mobile apps, so we care about the same things you do — retention, store ratings, performance and a clean experience. We keep scope honest, explain trade-offs in plain language and build apps with room to grow.

Frequently asked questions

Do you build apps for both Android and iOS?

Yes. We build for both platforms, either natively or with a cross-platform framework, depending on what suits the product.

How long does it take to build a mobile app?

It depends on scope. A focused MVP is usually a matter of weeks, while a larger product with many features, integrations and an admin panel takes longer. We share a timeline after understanding your requirements.

Can you publish the app on Google Play and the App Store?

Yes. We help prepare the store listing, privacy details and builds, and handle the review submission process. The app is published under your own developer accounts.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work with clients in India and internationally, communicating over email and video calls.

Tell us about the problem you are solving. We will reply with honest questions, a suggested approach and next steps.
