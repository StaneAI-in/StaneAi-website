AI Chatbot & Assistant Development

A good chatbot answers real questions correctly, admits when it doesn’t know and hands over to a person smoothly. StaneAI builds AI chatbots and assistants grounded in your own information — for your website, mobile app or messaging channels.

What we build

  • Customer support chatbots that answer questions from your FAQs, policies and product information.
  • Lead and enquiry assistants that collect details and book follow-ups.
  • Internal assistants that help your team search documents, SOPs and knowledge bases.
  • In-app assistants built into mobile and web products.

Built on your information

Generic chatbots guess. We connect the assistant to your own approved content — website pages, documents, product data — so answers are based on what your business actually says. When a question falls outside that content, the bot should say so or pass the conversation to a human, not invent an answer.

What a good chatbot needs

  • A clear job — the specific questions or tasks it should handle.
  • Good source content that is accurate and kept up to date.
  • Human handover for complaints, edge cases and anything sensitive.
  • Review and improvement — reading real conversations to fix gaps.
  • Privacy by design — collecting only what is needed and being clear with users.

Frequently asked questions

Can the chatbot answer from our own documents?

Yes. We connect the chatbot to your approved content such as FAQs, policies, product information and documents, so its answers are grounded in your information.

Can the chatbot work in Hindi or other languages?

Modern AI models handle many languages, including Hindi and Hinglish. We test the languages your customers actually use before launch.

What happens when the chatbot doesn’t know the answer?

It should say so and offer a way to reach a person, such as email, a callback request or live chat handover. We design this path from the start.
