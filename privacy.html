Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StaneAI Private Limited (“StaneAI”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you visit staneai.in (the “Website”). We have written it to match how the Website actually works: it is a simple informational website with no user accounts, no sign-up forms, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

1. Information we collect

a) Information you choose to send us

If you email us (for example at contact@staneai.in or support@staneai.in), we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and anything you write in your message or attach to it.

b) Information collected automatically by our service providers

  • Hosting: The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and to operate the service. This is handled under GitHub’s own privacy statement.
  • Fonts: The Website loads fonts from Google Fonts. To deliver them, your browser connects to Google’s servers, which receive your IP address and basic browser information. This is handled under Google’s privacy policy.

c) Information stored on your own device

We do not use cookies. The Website stores a single setting (intro-seen) in your browser’s session storage so the opening animation is shown only once per visit. It contains no personal information, never leaves your device, and is deleted automatically when you close the browser tab or window.

2. How we use information

  • To read and reply to your messages and enquiries.
  • To discuss possible projects, partnerships or services you ask us about.
  • To keep the Website secure and working properly.
  • To comply with applicable law.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Sharing of information

We share information only where necessary: with service providers who help us run the Website and our email (such as GitHub, Google and our email provider), when required by law or a lawful request from authorities, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

4. How long we keep information

We keep emails and related information only for as long as needed to respond to you, continue a conversation or business relationship you started, or meet legal requirements. After that, we delete it.

5. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian law, you may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update your personal data;
  • erase your personal data, where we are not required to keep it;
  • withdraw any consent you have given; and
  • address any grievance you have about how we handle your data.

To make a request, email support@staneai.in. We will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

6. Children

The Website is meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

The Website is served only over encrypted HTTPS and uses a strict content security policy. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information we receive.

8. Links to other websites

The Website may link to other websites or platforms. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please read their policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it was last changed.
