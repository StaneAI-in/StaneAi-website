AI & Workflow Automation

A lot of everyday work is copying data between tools, sorting emails, updating sheets and writing the same replies. StaneAI builds automations — with AI where it genuinely helps — so your team can spend time on work that needs people.

What we automate

  • Data entry and syncing between forms, spreadsheets, CRMs and databases.
  • Email and document handling — reading invoices, forms or emails and pulling out the important fields.
  • Lead and enquiry routing — sorting incoming requests and sending them to the right person.
  • Reports and summaries generated automatically on a schedule.
  • Notifications and follow-ups on WhatsApp, email or chat tools.

Where AI fits — and where it doesn’t

Many automations need no AI at all: a clear rule (“when a form is submitted, add a row and send an email”) is faster, cheaper and more reliable. AI is useful when the input is messy — free-text emails, scanned documents, customer messages — and someone would otherwise have to read and understand it. We use AI for those steps and keep simple rules everywhere else, with human review wherever a mistake would be costly.

How we work

  1. Map the process — what happens today, step by step, and where time is lost.
  2. Pick the highest-value step — automate one thing well before automating everything.
  3. Build and test with real examples from your work.
  4. Monitor — logs and alerts so failures are noticed, not silent.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of businesses can use workflow automation?

Any business that repeats the same digital steps every day — agencies, clinics, shops, startups, service companies and back-office teams are common examples.

Is my data safe when AI is used?

We design automations to send only the data that is needed, prefer providers with clear data-protection terms, and discuss where data is stored and processed before building.

Do I need to replace my existing tools?

Usually not. Most automations connect the tools you already use through their APIs or integrations.
